# BeeTrust Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026

BeeTrust is an independently verifiable skills package maintained in the BeeTrust project repository:

https://github.com/dengcong80/1010GlobalAISkills/tree/main/beetrust-plugin

## Information handled

BeeTrust does not operate a hosted account system, advertising service, analytics service, or customer database. The package processes the prompts, fixtures, and evidence objects supplied to its host runtime. The local demonstration does not require a BeeTrust account or send data to a BeeTrust server.

When the customs workflow is explicitly run with live-source lookup enabled, it retrieves a public New Zealand Customs page. It does not submit customs declarations or transmit a shipment to a government system.

## User responsibility

Do not provide personal information, credentials, private laboratory reports, unreleased shipment records, or commercially sensitive data unless the host platform and the user’s organisation have approved that processing. The host platform may process prompts and files under its own privacy terms.

## Retention and contact

The project team does not promise retention, backup, or deletion services for data stored by the host platform or by a user’s local environment. Privacy questions can be raised through the project support channel:

https://github.com/dengcong80/1010GlobalAISkills/issues

This policy describes the BeeTrust package and does not replace the privacy policy of the platform where it is installed.
