# BeeTrust Terms of Use

Effective date: 27 September 2026

## Permitted use

BeeTrust may be used for evaluation, education, internal prototyping, workflow design, and evidence-review demonstrations. The package is provided with its source, fixtures, and tests so users can inspect and verify the implementation.

## Important limitations

BeeTrust is decision-support software. It does not issue an MPI certificate, customs ruling, laboratory certificate, UMF licence, legal opinion, broker instruction, or approval to release goods. HS classifications, tariff assumptions, market-access rules, laboratory interpretations, and release decisions must be checked against current official sources and approved by the responsible exporter, broker, laboratory, compliance officer, or regulator.

The demonstration uses synthetic trade fixtures and versioned rule snapshots. It is not a claim about a particular shipment, customer, realised saving, or regulatory outcome.
